THE HENLEY

Make yourself at home.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Choose a floor to see inside, click a floor for room details, or click a door to open it.

In walkthrough, use WASD and the mouse. E operates the door you are looking at. Escape releases the mouse; Exit Walkthrough returns to presentation.

Room layout follows the supplied DWH plan. Heights, undimensioned rooms, stair geometry and furniture are inferred. Furnishings and upgraded finishes are illustrative.

Room dimensions are maximum marketing measurements. Wall fits, bays and roof are approximate. The 6 m stair reference is not treated as the physical stair run. Plan view and Measure inspect actual model geometry.
